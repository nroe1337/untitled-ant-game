The hunger loop

Queen lays eggs, larvae eat food, workers hatch, foragers bring food back. Every worker also eats, so growth always pulls on food. If food runs out, workers starve one by one, leaving only the queen to rebuild from.

The learning loop

Elders turn workers into knowledge. Research spends it to unlock jobs, chambers, techniques and whole screens. The Hall of elders raises how much knowledge you can hold, which gates the bigger techs.

The surface loop

Ten chambers and Upward tunnels open the surface. Expeditions bring loot that pays for research and fortifications, reveal the map, and eventually find the rival colony.

The lineage loop

Fungus feeds larvae and becomes royal jelly. Jelly breeds castes that farm, store, fight and weave. A big colony earns Lineage when its flight leaves, and Lineage perks make every new colony grow faster.

The war loop

You provoke raids when you are ready. Clean wins pay chitin, which builds husk halls and unlocks campaigns. Campaigns take map tiles, and tiles pay territory yields back to the colony.
